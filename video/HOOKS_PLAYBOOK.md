# Hook-first playbook (Meddy's rule, Nov 3 2026)
Applies to every post on both brands (NovaProHome 6916320 and Run It With AI 7786768).
Goal: more people stop, watch, comment and come back. Titles must be attractive and about the PERSON, not about the product or service.

## Rules
0. **Meddy, Nov 3: no unrelated opener.** The video starts directly on the topic in its title/description: no separate joke card, intro or "before we start" segment. The hook is the first line of the actual lesson (said over the real content), not a stand-alone card.
1. **First line / first 2 seconds = the hook.** Use one of these:
   - Joke or relatable moment ("Your router is in a closet. Your camera is in the backyard. They are not on speaking terms.")
   - Trend or calendar moment (clocks falling back, porch-pirate season, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, first freeze, a trending meme format)
   - Curiosity gap ("Nobody expects the alarm to fix the Wi-Fi")
   - Bold, true statement ("It's always the Wi-Fi.")
2. **Titles sell the feeling or the problem, not the brand.** Product names go later in the caption, never as the first words of the title.
   Bad: "Ring Floodlight Cam Pro install, Reston". Good: "It turned on for every raccoon. Now it shows their face."
3. **End with an easy question** people want to answer (an opinion, a story, a "which one are you"). One question, not three.
4. **Pin or reply fast:** answer every comment within the day; the first hour matters most.
5. **Honest hooks only** (this is what keeps reach long-term):
   - The video must deliver what the title promises in the first seconds. No bait-and-switch, no fake "shocking" thumbnails, no made-up stories or numbers.
   - No "comment YES", "tag 3 friends", "share to win" or "like if…" bait. Facebook and Instagram demote these. "Share with a neighbor who needs this" is fine.
   - No politics or tragedy jokes. Veterans Day and similar days stay respectful.
6. Keep the existing rules: AI labels, affiliate disclosure, UTM links, no phone on YouTube, customer privacy.

## Follower growth (Nov 4 2026)
Baseline Nov 3: NovaProHome YouTube 5, Facebook 9, TikTok 4, LinkedIn 4; Run It With AI YouTube 1. Total 23.
Problem: posts get views (FB reels ~578/week, TikTok ~622/week) but almost nobody follows. Viewers get no reason to follow.
Rules from now on:
1. **Give a reason to follow, every post.** Last line of every video and caption: one specific follow ask tied to what's next ("Follow, part 2 shows the wiring tomorrow", "Subscribe, Friday is the money lesson"). Not a generic "like & subscribe".
2. **Series beat one-offs.** Group posts into numbered series ("Doorbell Fixes 1/5", "AI Money Moves 1/12"). People follow to see the next part.
3. **Answer comments the same day**, and turn good questions into the next video ("You asked…").
4. **Pin a first comment** on YouTube with the closing question.
5. 30-day targets (by Dec 4): NovaProHome YT 30, FB 60, TikTok 40, LinkedIn 60; Run It With AI 50.
