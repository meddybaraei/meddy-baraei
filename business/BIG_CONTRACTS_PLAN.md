SAM.gov industry codes (NAICS) to select:
- **561621** Security Systems Services (except Locksmiths) ← primary
- **238210** Electrical Contractors and Other Wiring Installation Contractors (low-voltage wiring)
- **238290** Other Building Equipment Contractors
- (later, once licensed for it) EV chargers fall under 238210 as well.

Watch out: companies that charge to "register you in SAM" are resellers. SAM.gov is free.

## Phase 3 — Licenses for bigger jobs (month 2–3)
- [ ] **DPOR contractor license**: you have the Class A/B/C packages (Oct 14). Single jobs over the Class C limit need **Class B**; very large ones need **Class A**. → Meddy
- [ ] **Electrician license** (DPOR tradesman application in progress) → unlocks EV chargers, generators, line-voltage work.

- [ ] Optional later: **BICSI** cabling certification (data centers love it).

## Phase 4 — Go get the work (start in parallel from week 2)
1. **Subcontracting for big firms (biggest opportunity: Loudoun "Data Center Alley")**
   Targets: M.C. Dean, Rosendin, Convergint, Johnson Controls, Securitas Technology, Dynalectric and other NoVA electrical/security integrators.
   Ask to be added to their subcontractor/vendor list for low-voltage, camera and cabling work. → Claude finds contacts + sends outreach (with your OK).
2. **Small government purchases** (about $10,000 and under, often no formal bid): introduce NovaProHome to facilities managers at county buildings, schools, libraries, parks. → Claude drafts.
3. **HOAs & condo associations** (through their management companies): amenity cameras, gate/pool access, smart locks. → Claude outreach.
4. **Apartment communities & builders** (smart locks per unit, new-home prewire): NoVA builders such as Van Metre, Stanley Martin, Toll Brothers, NVR. → Claude outreach.
5. **Bid alerts**: Claude checks SAM.gov, eVA, Loudoun & Fairfax bid boards weekly for NAICS 561621/238210 and sends you only the realistic ones.
