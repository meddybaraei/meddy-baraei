# New YouTube channel: AI for retail store owners (revised Oct 31 2026)

## Who it's for (one avatar)
Owners and managers of **small retail businesses that sell products**: furniture and mattress stores, appliance, flooring, home décor, boutiques and gift shops. One to five locations, 2 to 50 staff.
Their problem: "Everyone says use AI. I don't know where to start, I don't have time, and I don't want to break anything."
Our promise: **"Start using AI in your store this week, step by step. No tech background needed."**

## Our edge
- Meddy has real retail operations experience: store performance reviews, sales and cancellation tracking, staff, deliveries.
- His playbooks and training material get **rewritten into new, general versions**. Nothing confidential leaves his employer.
- Every video shows a real, repeatable workflow in Claude (and its connectors: Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Sheets, Canva, social tools), not hype.

## Rule for Meddy's material (important)
Only use material Meddy owns or that is public. Anything from an employer gets rebuilt from scratch: no company names, logos, store names, real sales figures, customer data or internal documents. We teach the *method*; numbers in videos are clearly-labeled examples.

## Format and schedule (12 months, 2 videos a week + Shorts)
- **Tuesday, the main lesson (8–12 min, 16:9):** one complete workflow, start to finish.
- **Friday, quick win (4–6 min):** one prompt or one connector, done in 5 minutes.
- **3 Shorts a week** cut from the two videos (hook, the one trick, result).
- **Monthly:** "AI Store Challenge". Viewers try that month's workflow and comment results, and we feature the best in the next video. This builds community and momentum.
- **Free download** in every description: the prompt pack for that month (a lead magnet, later an email list).

## Production: 100% AI-made, near-zero cost
- **Script:** Claude writes it from Meddy's rebuilt playbooks, and it's fact-checked.
- **Voice:** AI voice (Gemini TTS, cents per video). Each description says "narrated with an AI voice."
- **Visuals:** our render tools (`video/tools/`) for titles, step lists, diagrams and clean mockups of chats and screens, with no real customer data on screen. AI images only where they help. No realistic fake footage.
- **YouTube rules:** each video teaches something specific and has its own script and visuals. No templated filler, since YouTube refuses ads on mass-produced or repetitive AI content. Affiliate links carry a disclosure.
