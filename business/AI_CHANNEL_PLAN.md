# New YouTube channel: AI tools for small business (Meddy picked option 3, Oct 31 2026)

## Positioning (one avatar)
"AI for small business" is crowded. We narrow it to **owners of home-service and trades businesses**: installers, electricians, HVAC, plumbers, cleaners, handymen, contractors. They are busy, not technical, and pay for tools that save hours.
Our edge: every video shows a real workflow from running an actual installer business (NovaProHome): quotes, follow-ups, social posts, reviews, permits and bids. These are real use cases, not hype.

## Production: 100% AI-made, near-zero cost
- **Script:** written by Claude from real workflows, then fact-checked (prices, features, links).
- **Voice:** AI voice. Gemini TTS, a few cents per video, within the $10/month Gemini cap.
- **Visuals:** our own render tools (`video/tools/`) for title cards, step lists, diagrams, screen-style mockups and charts. AI images only where they add something. No AI "real-looking" footage, so no realistic-synthetic label is needed. Still say "narrated with an AI voice" in each description.
- **Formats:**
  - Long video (8–12 min, 16:9): one complete workflow, e.g. "How I answer every lead in 5 minutes with AI".
  - 2 Shorts cut from each long video (30–50 s, vertical).
- **Cadence for the 4-week test:** 1 long video + 2 Shorts per week, so 4 long videos and 8 Shorts.

## YouTube rules we follow (to stay monetizable)
- Every video teaches one specific, useful thing. No templated filler or re-uploaded content.
- Each video has its own script, visuals and examples ("inauthentic / mass-produced" content gets refused for ads).
- Disclose AI narration. Label any realistic AI footage as altered or synthetic.
- Affiliate links carry a disclosure in the description.
