## 5. Make each customer worth more ($1 to $100)
| Lever | NovaProHome version |
|---|---|
| Increase price | Bundle pricing instead of per-device prices. Stop discounting multi-device jobs below cost. |

| High-ticket | EV charger installs (needs an electrical license or a licensed electrician partner); commercial camera systems after licensing |

## 6. Selling on calls and site visits: CLOSER
1. **C**larify why they're here: "What made you reach out now?"
2. **L**abel the problem: "So right now X, you want Y, and Z is in the way?"
3. **O**verview past pain: "What have you tried? How did that go?" (DIY, a handyman, an app that never worked)
4. **S**ell the vacation: what life looks like after. "Your phone shows who's at the door, packages are on camera, nothing to manage."
5. **E**xplain away concerns: time ("one visit"), money (fixed written price, downsell), fit ("works with what you have"), past bad installers ("engineer-run, clean work, guaranteed")
6. **R**einforce the decision: confirm the date, send a written summary and what to expect, then follow up after the job and ask for a review.
