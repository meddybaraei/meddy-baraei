# NovaProHome: $10k a month (started Nov 7 2026)
Source: Alex Hormozi on *The Diary of a CEO* with Steven Bartlett (youtu.be/Kl-I7sUcAOY, 3 h 14 min; Gemini watched it on Nov 7). These are his frameworks applied to our business, in our own words. Builds on business/MILLION_PLAN.md.

## 1. The math (prices in MILLION_PLAN are still waiting on Meddy's approval)
| Stream | Per month | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| "Whole-Home Security in One Visit" (~$799 labor) | 7 jobs | $5,593 |
| Starter (doorbell + 1 camera + chime, ~$349) | 6 jobs | $2,094 |
| Add-ons on those jobs (lock, floodlight, mesh point, ~$150 avg) | 8 | $1,200 |

| **Total** | ~13 jobs | **≈ $10,080** |
At roughly a 30% close rate that's **about 45 quote requests a month, or 11 a week**. Every action below either adds leads or makes each job worth more.
Big jobs like the Ashburn full-home system (5 cameras + doorbell + 16-sensor alarm) count as 2–3 normal jobs. Two of those a month cuts the leads needed by a third.
⚠️ Cameras and alarms are where the money is, so the DCJS license (MILLION_PLAN §0) is a $10k blocker, not a side task.

## 2. The 4 Rs: grow from customers we already have (Hormozi: Retain, Review, Refer, Resell)
- **Retain:** text 3 days after every job ("Everything working? Any alerts bugging you?"), then a 30-day check-in. Offer the $199/yr Health Check at handoff.
- **Review:** ask the same day, before leaving, with the link: https://g.page/r/CeeJDbkRDpk2EAE/review. Ask them to "mention what we installed and your town". Goal: 20 Google reviews by Dec 31.
- **Refer:** "Who else on your street has a doorbell that doesn't work?" Get 1–2 names. No paid rewards (Meddy's rule). The *friend* gets a free 15-minute Wi-Fi/doorbell check.
- **Resell:** at every handoff, offer the next device: doorbell → lock or floodlight → mesh point → alarm. The Ashburn job shows how big a full-home system can be.

## 3. Speed to lead
Hormozi: whoever answers first usually wins. Target: **reply to every quote request within 5 minutes** during work hours (an auto-text if Meddy is on a ladder), and a call within 1 hour. The email session handles first replies; Meddy calls.

## 4. The 3 Ds: build the business so it doesn't depend only on Meddy
Document → Demonstrate → Duplicate. Turn every install type into a 1-page checklist (doorbell, floodlight, alarm, thermostat, lock, mesh). The job videos already show each step, so they double as training. When volume passes about 15 jobs a month, hire one tech who is a **"barrel"**: someone who can finish a job alone, not just help.

## 5. The 3 Ps: pick what to sell and say
Pain (what homeowners hate), Past profession (Meddy is an electrical engineer, so he can do wiring, transformers, Wi-Fi), Passion. Our edge is "engineered, not just installed". Lead with jobs other installers won't do: old intercoms, transformers, dead Wi-Fi zones, full-home systems.

## 6. Experiment, don't guess
Run 1 test at a time for 2 weeks and compare quote requests per week. Order: (a) "Whole-Home in One Visit" offer vs a Starter offer in Google Business Profile posts; (b) a founder-story post vs a tip post on Facebook; (c) the quote form with photos vs without.
**Weekly scoreboard (Monday review):** quote requests · quotes sent · jobs won · revenue · reviews · followers.

## 7. Avoid "the woman in the red dress" (shiny objects)
One offer, one avatar, one channel for 90 days (MILLION_PLAN §2). New ideas go on a list, not into this month's work.

## 8. Content techniques taken from the video (also in video/HOOKS_PLAYBOOK.md)
- **Open loop:** promise the payoff, then make them wait for it ("The #1 reason doorbells fail is… let's go on a ladder"). The opening is still the topic itself (Meddy's no-unrelated-opener rule).
- **Packaged frameworks:** give tips short memorable names ("The 3-Point Front Door Check", "The 4 Rs").
- **Fast start, then slow down:** cut every 1–2 seconds in the first 10 seconds, then let the lesson breathe.
- **Honesty about struggle builds trust:** Meddy's real story (engineer → his own business, the hard parts). Only true details from Meddy.
- **Say the goal out loud:** "We're a small local business trying to reach 100 subscribers by Dec 31. If this helped, subscribing helps a lot." Honest and specific; no fake reverse psychology.
