What we do

We help people turn their own life and skills into a small business they own.

Seedline is a guided AI coach for beginners who have never used AI and never run a business: immigrants, retail and sales workers, people who sell things on the side. It speaks English, Spanish, Persian and Arabic and works on a phone.

  1. 1 · TalkAnswer 5 questions, upload a résumé or photos of your work
  2. 2 · StrengthsWhat you're good at, in your own words
  3. 3 · 3 honest ideasWith cost, time, risk and a weekend test
  4. 4 · Launch planStep by step, with local rules for your ZIP code
  5. 5 · My dayA daily routine around work, family and health

The problem

  • Most new owners have little to spend. In one survey of 7,413 new business owners, about 90% planned under $200 a month on marketing and 26% planned $0.
  • About 20–22% of new businesses close in their first year.
  • The help that exists is in English, generic, or sold with income promises. Since 2023 the FTC has brought at least six cases against sellers who promised people a money-making business, several of them using AI.

Sources: UENI, Aug 2026 (not a representative sample) · SCORE · FTC, Automators AI

What's built today

  • The full journey above, plus a launch kit: business names, ready-to-send messages, a flyer and a price helper.
  • A checked list of Virginia setup rules with official links, and a one-page summary to bring to a free SBDC or SCORE advisor.
  • Weekly check-ins, milestones, a "pause a week" button and a wins jar.
  • Personal AI agents, a portfolio and a résumé builder with PDF and Word download.
  • One assistant that can act in the app, but only after the person taps "Do it".

Built and tested on phone screens in all 4 languages. No real users yet.

Why it's different

  • Starts from the person, not from a business idea they don't have yet.
  • Local rules for where they live, with the office to confirm with.
  • Persian and Arabic, right-to-left, alongside English and Spanish.
  • Honest by design: no income promises, reality checks on every idea, and advice to keep the day job until they're ready.
  • They own their data: it stays on their phone, with one-tap download or delete.

The closest product, Wix Business Launcher, turns a résumé into ideas but leads into a website subscription and works mainly in English.

The pilot plan

  • 4–6 weeks, free, with 20–40 people from 2–3 community organizations and a few local groups.
  • We measure: how many finish a plan, take a real first step, come back in week 2–3, and would pay for the next level.
  • We form the company and build the full site (with accounts and Telegram) only if the pilot shows people use it.
