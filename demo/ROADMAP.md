# Seedline 5-day build plan (Oct 14–18, 2026)

Each day: build the day's items in `demo/story-to-business.html`, test every flow on a phone-sized screen in all 4 languages (with mocked AI), publish the demo to the same link, update the test page and the comparison page if needed, commit and push to `research/idea-to-business-platform`, and write a short note under "Daily log" below.

Rules that never change: no income promises, never the word "passive", honest reality checks, clear prices with easy cancel, the user owns their data, everything works in English, Spanish, Persian and Arabic.

## Day 1 — Wed Oct 14: Smarter, more honest ideas
- "Why not these?" — show 2 ideas we ruled out and why (fits budget, car, comfort with selling).
- Compare the 3 ideas side by side (time to first customer, cost, hours per week, risk).
- Use every profile answer in the reality check (budget, transport, home vs out).
- Home page: "How Seedline is different" section linking to the comparison page (no fake reviews).

## Day 2 — Thu Oct 15: Launch kit people can use today
- Business name choices with a quick check list (search the state business registry, domain, social handles).
- Ready-to-send first-customer messages (text, WhatsApp, Facebook post, flyer text) in their language, with copy buttons.
- A simple one-page flyer they can screenshot and share.
- Price helper: what to charge, explained, based on their area.

## Day 3 — Fri Oct 16: Local rules done right
- Curated Virginia starter list (state registration, county/city business license, tax registration, EIN free from the IRS) with official links and a "last checked" date; AI fills other states and says "confirm with this office".
- "Bring this to your free advisor" one-page summary for SBDC/SCORE meetings.
- Free local help finder by ZIP (SBDC, SCORE, library, community organizations).

## Day 4 — Sat Oct 17: Staying with them every week
- Weekly check-in (3 questions: what worked, what got stuck, what's next) that updates the plan.
- Milestones: first conversation, first test, first customer, first review; celebrate partial progress.
- "Pause a week" for life events, with no penalties.
- Progress page that shows how far they've come.

## Day 5 — Sun Oct 18: Polish and share
- Full review of all 4 languages, accessibility (keyboard, screen readers, contrast) and phone layout.
- Update the test page for all new features.
- One-page investor and partner summary.
- Final summary for Meddy of everything built and what's next.
