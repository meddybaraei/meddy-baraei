# Unit Economics, Pricing Models and Go-to-Market for an "Idea to Launched Business" Platform (Home-Services Wedge)

Research date: 2026-10-12. US focus. Note on source quality: many benchmark figures came back only through secondary vendor or agency blogs that cite primary reports (LocaliQ/WordStream, ServiceTitan, BLS). localiq.com and ueni.com could not be fetched directly from this environment (DNS failure), so primary-report verification was not possible for those. Figures are flagged with date and confidence.

## Pricing models in market and their benchmarks (SaaS, setup fees, % of ad spend, retainers, pay-per-lead, revenue share/equity)

### Takeaway
The market has a clear price ladder. AI site builders charge about $12–95/mo. White-label CRM/marketing stacks (GoHighLevel) charge $97–497/mo. Ad management is 10–20% of spend, usually with a floor of about $750–1,500/mo for small local accounts. Lead marketplaces charge $10–150+ per shared lead. Studios take about 10–60% equity, averaging about 34%. A home-services wedge most naturally combines a setup fee, a software subscription, and either a %-of-spend fee with a minimum or a per-booked-job fee. Equity/rev-share only fits a small, selected subset.

### Cited Findings
**SaaS subscriptions**
- Durable: the free tier gives only a Durable subdomain and no custom domain. Paid tiers are Starter $15/mo ($12 annual), Business $25/mo ($20 annual) and Mogul $95/mo ($80 annual) per TechRadar. A July 2026 post reports the same tiers — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/software-services/durable); [dailyaifixs, Jul 2026](https://dailyaifixs.com/blog/durable-pricing-2026-free-is-just-a-subdomain). A Sept 2026 comparison lists $25/$49 monthly instead, which conflicts with the others — [web3aiblog](https://www.web3aiblog.com/blog/best-ai-website-builders-2026).
- GoHighLevel (2026): Starter $97/mo, Unlimited $297/mo, Agency Pro (SaaS Mode, unlimited sub-accounts, rebilling, white-label resale) $497/mo. SMS, email, voice and AI are billed on top through usage-based "wallet" credits. Sources are mostly affiliate blogs — [ecosire](https://ecosire.com/fr/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-worth-it-2026); [vantaige](https://vantaige.io/ai-tool/gohighlevel).
- Wix: I did not retrieve current pricing (see Gaps).

**% of ad spend / retainers**
- The typical PPC management fee is 10–20% of monthly ad spend. Some agencies use tiered rates, with a higher % on small budgets — [AgencyAnalytics](https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/ppc-pricing).
- For small local budgets, many agencies switch to flat retainers because a % fee is either too low to be profitable for the agency or too high for the client. Basic local retainers start at about $750–1,200/mo and mid-tier at $1,500–3,500/mo. One illustrative structure is "15% of spend, $1,500 minimum." These are vendor-sourced, 2026-dated, not a neutral survey — [clicksgeek](https://clicksgeek.com/ppc-management-services-cost/).

**Pay-per-lead / pay-per-appointment marketplaces**
- Angi Leads: an annual fee of about $288–300 plus $15–85 per lead, depending on trade. Roofing, HVAC and remodeling sit at the high end. One source estimates an effective cost of $1,400+ per booked job. Contracts auto-renew for 12 months, with 30–35% early-cancellation penalties. Angi switched to a "homeowner choice" model in January 2025, and its network revenue reportedly fell 79% in Q4 — [Valley Marketing Group, 2026](https://thevalleymarketinggroup.com/blog/angi-vs-thumbtack-vs-google-lsa-contractors-2026/); [automatethejourney](https://automatethejourney.com/firstlap/blog/are-angi-thumbtack-leads-worth-it).
- Thumbtack: about $8 per lead for cleaning, rising to $150+ for kitchen remodels and big HVAC installs. Common complaints are being charged for leads that never respond and leads shared among several contractors — [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/is-thumbtack-a-scam/); [sidehusl](https://sidehusl.com/thumbtack/).
- Shared marketplace leads close at about 6–10%, versus 18–24% for organic search leads. This comes from a vendor blog — [automatethejourney](https://automatethejourney.com/firstlap/blog/are-angi-thumbtack-leads-worth-it).
- In 2023 the FTC finalized an order requiring HomeAdvisor (an Angi affiliate) to pay up to $7.2M over deceptive claims about lead quality — [adaptdigitalsolutions](https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/homeadvisor-vs-angieslist-vs-houzz-vs-porch-vs-thumbtack-vs-yelp-vs-bark/).
- Google LSA is pay-per-lead. Its CPL is covered in the home-services section below.

**Revenue share / equity**
- Venture studio stakes range from 15–80% of initial equity, averaging about 34%. Operators commonly cite 20–60%, and some studios take only 10%. Advisors warn that a stake above 60% makes the founder effectively an employee. These sources are studio marketing content — [ninetwothree](https://ninetwothree.co/blog/how-do-venture-studios-make-money); [ecosistemastartup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/venture-building-53-irr-vs-21-vc-tradicional-en-startups/); [dovetailstudios](https://dovetailstudios.com/articles/venture-studio-faqs).
- Rank-and-rent sites rent for a flat monthly fee, a per-lead fee or a revenue share. Quoted rentals are $1,000–2,000/mo per site, but this is unsourced vendor content — [prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/lead-gen-business-model).

**Retention-relevant pricing evidence**
- Vendasta's analysis of 100k+ SMB accounts found that SMBs sold only one product retain about 30% at 2 years. Weekly engagement raises retention by 26% and regular proof-of-performance reports raise it by 38%. Vendasta is a vendor with a platform to sell — [Vendasta churn study](https://www.vendasta.com/blog/vendasta-client-churn-study/).

### Inferences
- **SaaS-only ($15–100/mo)** is cheap to acquire but price-anchored low by Durable and Wix. Churn is high for idea-stage users, and gross margin is high but ARPU is low.
- **Setup fee ($500–3,000)** recovers human onboarding cost and filters out tire-kickers. Its weakness is friction at the idea stage.
- **% of spend (10–20%)** scales with success. On a $1,000/mo budget, though, 15% is only $150, so a minimum (about $500–1,500) is needed. That minimum then prices out most idea-stage users.
- **Pay-per-booked-job** aligns best with home-services owners, who resent Angi and Thumbtack. It carries ad-spend risk, so it needs tight trade and geo selection. It is a credible differentiator ("exclusive leads, not shared").
- **Equity/rev-share** needs selection (venture-studio style). It is illiquid and slow to pay off for local service businesses that will never exit, and a rev-share is hard to audit for cash-based trades.
- Vendasta's data on one-product retention implies that bundling (site + CRM + ads + reporting) is a retention lever, not just an ARPU lever.

### Gaps
- Current Wix pricing and Wix AI site-builder tiers were not retrieved.
- There is no audited data on pay-per-booked-appointment pricing (for example, a % of job value) from independent operators.
- Atlas-style "founder-as-a-service" rev-share terms for local businesses (as opposed to tech startups) were not found.

## Cost side: LLM/API and media generation, hosting/domain, support labor, ad-ops human-in-the-loop

### Takeaway
AI generation costs per launched business are small. A brand kit plus site copy plus about 20–50 images plus a few short video ads probably costs low tens of dollars at 2026 list prices. Video is the dominant AI cost. Human labor (onboarding, ad-ops, support) and ad spend dominate the cost of goods sold, not inference.

### Cited Findings
- Veo: Google retired the Veo 3 API on June 30, 2026 and replaced it with Veo 3.1. Quoted Veo 3.1 rates conflict: $0.20/sec standard and $0.10/sec Fast with no audio in one source, about $0.75/sec with audio on Vertex/Gemini API in another, and Veo 3.1 Lite at $0.05–0.08/sec in a third — [forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/text-to-video-api-comparison); [apiframe](https://apiframe.ai/blog/ai-video-api-pricing-2026); [aiagent-navi](https://aiagent-navi.com/special/video-gen-api-pricing/).
- Sora: OpenAI said the Sora 2 API would be removed on September 24, 2026, with no successor announced. As of this writing that date has passed, so the API should be treated as unavailable. Its prior pricing was about $0.10/sec at 720p ($0.05/sec batch) and Sora 2 Pro $0.30–0.70/sec — [apiframe](https://apiframe.ai/blog/ai-video-api-pricing-2026).
- Runway is credit-based. The approximate conversions are Gen-4 Turbo about $0.05/sec and Gen-4.5 about $0.12/sec. Runway's core models are silent, while Veo 3.1 and Sora 2 generate audio — [apiframe](https://apiframe.ai/blog/best-ai-video-generation-apis).
- HeyGen: only consumer tiers were found. Creator is $29/mo (1080p, 30 min), Pro from $49 and Business $149. API pricing was not found — [vpsranking](https://vpsranking.com/ai/heygen/).
- Images: gpt-image-1 costs about $0.011, $0.042 and $0.167 per 1024² image at low, medium and high quality. gpt-image-1.5 costs about $0.009, $0.034 and $0.133. gpt-image-1 is reportedly retiring on Oct 23, 2026 — [pricepertoken](https://pricepertoken.com/gpt-image-pricing). Gemini 3 Pro Image is quoted at about $0.134/image and Imagen 4 Fast at about $0.02/image by aggregator blogs (low confidence) — [tkmxai](https://tkmxai.it.com/decoding-ai-image-generation-api-pricing-in-2026).
- The GoHighLevel model shows that messaging, voice and AI usage costs are passed through via wallet credits, separate from the platform fee — [ecosire](https://ecosire.com/fr/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-worth-it-2026).

### Inferences
- Illustrative AI cost per launched business, built from the prices above and not a measured figure:
  - 3 × 15-second video ads with audio on Veo 3.1 at $0.20–0.75/sec is about $9–34.
  - 40 images at about $0.04 is about $1.60.
  - LLM text for brand, site and ad copy is likely under $5 at frontier-model rates (unverified, see Gaps).
  - The total is roughly $15–45 per launch, before regenerations. Iteration (2–5× regenerations) could push it to $50–150.
- Recurring AI cost (refreshing ad creative monthly) is probably $10–50/mo per client. That is small against a $300–1,500/mo price, so gross margin is driven by human ad-ops and support time.
- Vendor churn (Veo 3 retired, Sora API retired, gpt-image-1 retiring) argues for a model-agnostic generation layer.

### Gaps
- Official Anthropic, OpenAI and Google LLM text-token prices were not verified in this session. Check the official pricing pages.
- Domain (about $10–20/yr) and hosting costs were not sourced here. Typical static or edge hosting is near-zero marginal cost, but this is unverified.
- No benchmark was found for human ad-ops hours per SMB account per month, or for support cost per SMB account.
- HeyGen API (avatar video) per-minute pricing was not found.

## SMB agency churn, LTV/CAC, SMB ad budgets, new-business failure rate, and whether idea-stage users have budget

### Takeaway
Idea-stage founders mostly have little or no budget: about 90% of new owners in one 2026 dataset planned to spend under $200/mo. About 20–22% of new establishments close within year one, and about half survive five years. SMB agency clients churn fast when results lag: 67% of SMBs that outsourced marketing switched providers within six months. Paid-ads services therefore need existing operators with demand-capture budgets. Idea-stage users fit a low-cost SaaS or setup tier.

### Cited Findings
- New business survival from BLS BED data, via secondary sources: about 79.6% survive one year, 50.6% five years and 34.7% ten years — [SCORE, Dec 2025](https://www.score.org/greaterphoenix/resource/blog-post/small-business-failure-rates-2024-summary). The newer cohort (year ending March 2024/2025) shows 77.9% first-year survival and 51.4% five-year survival — [startbusinessbystate](https://startbusinessbystate.com/business-survival-rates-by-state); [axis-intelligence](https://axis-intelligence.com/small-business-failure-rate-statistics/). The 1994–2024 mean first-year survival is about 78.7% — [voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/startup-failure-statistics-2026).
- UENI onboarding data (Aug 2026, n=7,413 new business owners): 64.3% plan to spend $1–200/mo on marketing and 26% plan $0, so about 90.5% plan under $200/mo. These are stated plans and the sample is not representative. Seen via search snippet; the page itself could not be fetched — [UENI](https://ueni.com/blog/small-business-marketing-budget/).
- LocaliQ: businesses with 10 or fewer employees were 31% more likely to have a marketing budget under $500/mo — [LocaliQ SMB trends](https://localiq.com/blog/small-business-marketing-trends-report/).
- Intuit/QuickBooks 2025 estimates the average small-business ad budget at about $78,000/yr (about $6.5k/mo). The sample was marketing leaders, which likely skews larger — [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/advertising-trends-2025/); [AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/smb-tv-advertising-report-2025).
- Statista has 2025 SMB ad budgets by industry (n=1,006, March 2025), but the data is paywalled — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1620893/advertising-budget-industry-smbs-usa/).
- Agency churn: the Vendasta 2023 Agency Insights Report found most agencies see 2–8% churn (presumably monthly; the period was not specified in the snippet), and 15% didn't know their churn rate — [Duda blog](https://blog.duda.co/warning-signs-your-smb-client-might-churn-and-how-to-keep-them).
- vcita's April 2025 survey of 500 US SMBs found that 67% of SMBs that outsourced marketing in the past year switched providers within six months or less, mostly for lack of revenue results — [vcita](https://www.vcita.com/press/smb-survey-highlights-marketing-agency-churn).
- An agency churn table (18% annual churn for retainer agencies, 42% for project-based) circulates, but its sources cite each other and it is unverified — [agiled](https://agiled.app/statistics/client-retention-statistics).

### Inferences
- Rough LTV math, illustrative only. At $500/mo blended revenue, 70% gross margin and 5% monthly churn, the average lifetime is 20 months and LTV is about $7,000. At 8% monthly churn, LTV is about $4,400. Under a 3:1 LTV:CAC rule that supports a CAC of about $1,500–2,300 for existing operators. Idea-stage users at $20–50/mo SaaS with likely higher churn support a CAC under about $100–200, which means free or viral acquisition only.
- About 1 in 5 brand-new businesses die in year one, before compounding LTV. Idea-stage cohorts therefore carry structural churn on top of service churn.
- First-year ad-spend ROI for idea-stage businesses: no direct data was found. Given 90% planning under $200/mo, and home-services CPLs of $50–230 on search, most idea-stage owners could afford only about 1–4 search leads per month. That is too few to learn from. Meta (about $41 CPL) and LSA (about $50–70) are more viable starting channels.

### Gaps
- There is no published LTV/CAC benchmark specific to SMB marketing agencies or done-for-you launch services. Promethean Research and AgencyAnalytics survey data were not retrieved.
- There is no hard data on first-year ad ROI for new businesses.
- The churn period (monthly or annual) in the Vendasta 2–8% figure was not confirmed.

## Home-services marketing benchmarks (CPL by trade on Google/Meta/LSA, close rates, average job values, 2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Google Search CPL averages about $91 across home services (LocaliQ 2025: roofing about $228, HVAC about $85, plumbing about $76). LSA is cheaper at about $50–70 for plumbing and HVAC. Meta home-improvement leads cost about $41. Ticket sizes of about $1.2k–3.2k for HVAC and plumbing and $12–18k for roofing make the economics work at about 30–40% lead-to-job conversion. CPLs rose about 10.5% year over year for most advertisers.

### Cited Findings
- LocaliQ 2025 home services search benchmarks (3,211 US campaigns, Apr 2024–Mar 2025, via secondary sources): average CPL is $90.92, roofing $228.15, plumbing $76.40 and HVAC $84.92 — [foundrycro](https://foundrycro.com/blog/home-services-marketing-benchmarks-by-trade/); [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/hvac-roofing-plumbing-google-ads-cost/). 69% of home-services advertisers saw CPL increase, averaging +10.51% year over year — [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/home-service-ad-cost-inflation-statistics-2026/). Other secondary sites quote conflicting HVAC (about $45–129) and roofing (about $79) figures, likely metric confusion — [pipelineon stats](https://pipelineon.com/blog/home-service-marketing-statistics/).
- Branded search averages about $34 per lead, versus $124–228 for non-branded — [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/home-service-ad-cost-inflation-statistics-2026/).
- The older LocaliQ (2021) figures were HVAC AC $55.15 and heating $62.18 per lead. These are stale — [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-advertising-benchmarks/).
- Google LSA: SearchLight Digital's Feb 2026 study of 888 contractors put the blended LSA CPL at about $53, with electrical $39, HVAC $51, plumbing $57 and drain/sewer $59. A May 2026 snapshot was higher: plumbing $69.26 and electrical $49.02. Roofing LSA figures conflict ($50–95 versus $350–500, the latter likely all-channel) — [enrichlabs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/google-local-service-ads-complete-guide-2026); [pipelineon LSA](https://pipelineon.com/blog/google-local-services-ads-home-service/).
- House cleaning leads run about $35, or under $50, with no LSA-specific benchmark — [cubecreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/small-business-marketing/google-local-service-ads-worth-it).
- Meta: the WordStream 2025 home and home-improvement lead campaigns averaged $41.26 CPL, 5.22% CVR and $2.23 CPC. That is about 55% cheaper than Google's $90.92. There is no 2026 edition. Across all industries the leads-objective CPL is $27.66, up 20.94% year over year (secondary sources) — [theadspend](https://theadspend.com/benchmarks/facebook-leads-home-improvement); [admanage](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ads-cost-per-lead-benchmarks). A roofing Meta estimate of $50–150 is unsourced — [jobnimbus](https://www.jobnimbus.com/blog/how-to-get-roofing-leads-on-facebook).
- Close and book rates: the residential CSR call book rate is 65–75%, with top performers above 85% and weak ones below 50%. HVAC lead-to-job conversion is 30–40% (ServiceTitan 2024 Pulse, via blog). The plumbing book rate was 41.5% in Jan 2026 (SearchLight) — [ainora](https://ainora.lt/blog/hvac-service-call-statistics-2026); [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/hvac-plumbing-electrical-pricing-strategy/).
- Average tickets: HVAC repair rose from $818 (2021) to $1,205 (2025) per Housecall Pro. SearchLight Jan 2026 puts plumbing at $2,208/job and HVAC heating repair at $3,225. Roofing is $12,000–18,000 — [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/hvac-plumbing-roofing-pricing-strategy/); [ustechautomations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/home-services-financing-automation-2026).
- Speed-to-lead: responding within 5 minutes is claimed to make a close 21× more likely. This is unverified vendor content — [pipelineon](https://pipelineon.com/blog/home-service-marketing-benchmarks-2026/).

### Inferences
- Implied cost per booked job: plumbing at $76 CPL and about 40% conversion is about $190 per job against about $2.2k revenue. HVAC at $85 and 35% is about $240 against $1.2–3.2k. Roofing at $228 and maybe 10–20% close (not sourced) is about $1.1–2.3k against $12–18k. All are comfortably profitable, which supports a pay-per-booked-job or %-of-spend model in these trades.
- Cleaning has a low CPL but also low tickets and is recurring. A per-booked-job price would need to be small, so subscription fits better there.
- Speed-to-lead and call booking (AI voice or SMS follow-up) are high-leverage features, because book rates range from below 50% to above 85%.

### Gaps
- Primary LocaliQ 2025/2026 report tables could not be accessed. Remodeling and cleaning Google CPLs are not confirmed.
- Roofing close rates and remodeling job values were not sourced.
- No Northern Virginia / DC-metro specific CPL data was found. DMV CPCs are likely above national averages, but this is unverified.

## Go-to-market: how competitors acquired users, partnerships, and done-for-you lead-gen case studies

### Takeaway
The winners used a free, instant "aha" (Durable: a site in 30 seconds; Lovable: a viral community and builders), recurring-commission resellers (GoHighLevel: 40% recurring affiliate commission plus agencies white-labeling via SaaS Mode), and formation-moment partnerships (LegalZoom + Wix website at incorporation; Stripe Atlas perks marketplace). Rank-and-rent evidence is anecdotal but shows local lead-gen sites renting for $1–2k/mo.

### Cited Findings
- Durable: one month after its fall 2022 soft launch it had created 26,000 sites, and a year after launch about 6 million. These are sites generated, not users. The flow is a free button that generates a site in about 30 seconds, then sign-up, edit and share. Durable raised an $18M CAD ($14M USD) Series A, about $20M USD total. Founder James Clift previously grew VisualCV to about 4M users — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/?p=367519); [Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/ai-website-builder/).
- GoHighLevel: 40% recurring affiliate commission on $97, $297 and $497 plans (for example, about $118.80/mo on a $297 referral). It has a two-tier sub-affiliate structure, and the cookie length is reported as 30 or 90 days (conflicting). SaaS Mode lets agencies resell under their own brand, which turns agencies into a distribution channel — [thestackinsiders](https://www.thestackinsiders.com/blog/gohighlevel-affiliate-program); [ecosire](https://ecosire.com/fr/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-worth-it-2026).
- Lovable: ARR estimates run from about $300M (Sacra, Jan 2026) to $400M (reported March 2026) to a $500M run-rate claim (third-party, June 2026). It has nearly 8M users (late-2025 CEO statement) and 174k+ Discord members — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/lovable); [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/how-lovable-growth-reached-500m-arr-in-19-months); [demandsage](https://www.demandsage.com/lovable-statistics/).
- Stripe Atlas gives approved founders a Perks tab of partner discounts and credits (up to $100k in credits per Stripe docs). Stripe accepts partner applications — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas/partner-benefits); [Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/suggest-new-perks-or-become-an-atlas-partner). Bubble partnered with Atlas — [Bubble](https://bubble.io/blog/stripe-atlas-partnership/).
- LegalZoom partnered with Wix (2022) to give new businesses immediate access to a website built for their business type at the time of formation. LegalZoom also partners with Perplexity, and its partner program offers links, banners, co-branded landing pages and an embedded legal flow — [Artificial Lawyer](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2022/05/03/legalzoom-website-builder-wix-form-partnership/); [LegalZoom partners](https://legalzoom.com/partner-programs); [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35606543/legalzoom-launches-embedded-legal-services-flow-making-it-easy-for-partners-to-provide-additional-value-to-their-customers).
- Rank-and-rent: one operator on The Side Hustle Show runs 100+ sites, replaced his day-job income in about 6 months and reached six figures in about 12. Deals are a flat monthly fee, a per-lead fee or a revenue share (anecdotal) — [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/rank-and-rent/); [Empire Flippers podcast](https://empireflippers.com/rank-and-rent-explained). A "$2,000/mo from 15 hours" newsletter case also exists (anecdotal) — [nichesitegrowth](https://nichesitegrowth.beehiiv.com/p/rank-rent-2000mo-just-15-hours-work).

### Inferences
- For the idea-stage funnel, a free instant generator (brand + site preview) is table stakes because of Durable. Monetization comes later at domain connect, ads launch or a done-for-you upgrade.
- For the home-services wedge, the strongest distribution is likely B2B2SMB:
  - trade-supply houses and distributors
  - franchise and dealer networks (HVAC OEM dealer programs)
  - field-service software (Jobber/Housecall Pro) partner marketplaces
  - recurring affiliate payouts in the GHL style (30–40%) for consultants and bookkeepers
  - These channels are not evidenced in the sources; this is reasoning.
- A NovaProHome-style case study (before/after CPL, booked jobs) is the key proof asset, given that 67% of SMBs churn within six months without visible results.
- Formation-moment partners (LegalZoom, Stripe Atlas, SBDCs, banks) reach idea-stage users, who have low budgets. They suit the SaaS tier, not the ads-management tier.

### Gaps
- No data was found on SBA SBDC or Chamber of Commerce referral partnerships for vendors (SBDCs generally restrict vendor endorsements; unverified).
- No credible creator-led ("start a business" TikTok/YouTube) acquisition metrics were found.
- No audited rank-and-rent or local-lead-gen P&L was found.
- No CAC figures for Durable, GoHighLevel or Lovable were found.

## Evidence on idea-stage users vs existing small businesses as a customer segment

### Takeaway
Existing operators have budgets, measurable job values and proven demand: average ad budgets run in the thousands per month among established SMBs, and home-services tickets range from about $1.2k to $18k. Idea-stage owners overwhelmingly plan near-zero spend (about 90% under $200/mo) and face about a 20% first-year mortality. The evidence favors existing home-services operators for revenue and the idea-stage segment for top-of-funnel volume and low-touch SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- About 90.5% of 7,413 new business owners planned under $200/mo of marketing spend, and 26% planned $0 (Aug 2026) — [UENI](https://ueni.com/blog/small-business-marketing-budget/).
- The established-SMB average ad budget is about $78k/yr, from a sample skewed to marketing leaders — [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/advertising-trends-2025/).
- Micro businesses (10 or fewer employees) are 31% more likely to budget under $500/mo — [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/small-business-marketing-trends-report/).
- First-year survival is about 78–80%, and five-year survival about 50% — [SCORE](https://www.score.org/greaterphoenix/resource/blog-post/small-business-failure-rates-2024-summary).
- SMBs switch marketing providers quickly when revenue results are absent: 67% within six months or less — [vcita](https://www.vcita.com/press/smb-survey-highlights-marketing-agency-churn).
- Venture studios claim higher success rates through selection and support. This is studio marketing — [bundl](https://bundl.com/articles/why-venture-studio-startups-have-higher-long-term-success-rates).

### Inferences
- A segmented offer:
  - **Free / about $20–50/mo** self-serve launch kit for idea-stage users (brand, site, LLC/partner hand-offs).
  - **About $1–3k setup plus about $500–1,500/mo**, or a per-booked-job fee, for existing or newly-licensed home-services operators with a budget of at least about $1.5–3k/mo in ad spend.
  - **Selective equity/rev-share** only for founders the company would co-build (studio-style).
- "Idea-stage but licensed trade worker going independent" (for example, an HVAC tech leaving an employer) is a promising sub-segment. They have a skill, demand exists and tickets are high. Budget evidence for them specifically was not found.

### Gaps
- No direct willingness-to-pay survey comparing idea-stage and existing SMBs for done-for-you launch services was found.
- No success-rate data was found for businesses launched via AI builders (Durable, etc.) versus traditional launches.
