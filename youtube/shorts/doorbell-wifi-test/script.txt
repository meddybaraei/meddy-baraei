Before you drill a single hole for a video doorbell, test your Wi-Fi at the front door.

The doorbell lives outside, often behind brick or stucco, and that's exactly where Wi-Fi gets weak.

Here's how I check it. Stand right where the doorbell will go, connect your phone to your home Wi-Fi, and run a speed test.

If it's slow there, the doorbell will be slow too. Laggy live view, and late notifications.

After setup, open the Ring app, go to Device Health, and check the signal strength.

Weak signal? Move the router closer, or add a mesh point or an extender near the door.
