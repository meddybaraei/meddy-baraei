Thinking about a Level 2 EV charger at home? Here's what a proper install actually involves.

Step one is a load calculation. We check your electrical panel to see if it can handle the charger on top of everything else in your house.

That answer decides the cost. Panel capacity, how far the cable has to run, and whether you need a panel upgrade.

Step two is the permit. In Loudoun and Fairfax County, an EV charger needs an electrical permit and an inspection.

Skip it, and you could have problems with insurance or when you sell the house.

Step three, set up off-peak scheduling, so the car charges overnight when power often costs less.
