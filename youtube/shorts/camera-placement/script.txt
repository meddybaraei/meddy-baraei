Most people mount their security camera in the wrong spot. Here's where I put them.

First, go high, but not too high. Around eight to ten feet keeps it out of reach and still sees faces.

Second, cover the entry points. Front door, back door, and the driveway. That's where people actually come in.

Third, watch the sun. A camera facing east or west can get blinded at sunrise or sunset.

Fourth, check the Wi-Fi right where it mounts, before you drill.

And respect privacy. Don't point it into your neighbor's windows.
