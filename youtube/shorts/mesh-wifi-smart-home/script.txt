Camera dropping offline? Doorbell slow? The problem usually isn't the device. It's the Wi-Fi.

One router in a corner of the house can't reach the garage, the backyard, and the front door.

A mesh system fixes that. Several nodes work together as one network, with one name and one password.

Placement matters. Put each node in the open, about halfway between the router and the dead zone. Not in a closet, and not behind the TV.

If you can, connect the nodes with an Ethernet cable. A wired backhaul is faster and more stable than wireless.

And many smart home devices only connect on 2.4 gigahertz, so keep that band turned on.
