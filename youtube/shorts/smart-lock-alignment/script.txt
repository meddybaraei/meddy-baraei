Before you install a smart lock, do this ten-second test on your door.

Close the door and turn the deadbolt by hand.

If you have to push or pull the door to make it lock, a smart lock will struggle, jam, and drain its batteries.

The fix is alignment first. Adjust the strike plate or the hinges until the bolt slides in smoothly.

Then install the lock, and always keep a backup way in, like a key or a keypad code.
