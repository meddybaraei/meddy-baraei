Installing a smart thermostat? Do these three checks before you touch a single wire.

One. Turn off the power to your heating and cooling system at the breaker.

Not just at the thermostat. At the breaker.

Two. Take a clear photo of your old wiring before you disconnect anything.

The letter on each terminal tells you where every wire goes back.

Three. Look for a C wire. That's the common wire that gives the thermostat steady power.

No C wire? Some systems still work and some don't, so run your wiring through Google's compatibility checker before you buy.

And if you see thick wires joined with wire nuts, it may be a line-voltage system. That's a hard stop. Call a pro.
