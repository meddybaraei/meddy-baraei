The most common smart lighting mistake? Putting smart bulbs on a regular wall switch.

Someone flips the switch off, the bulb loses power, and every schedule and voice command stops working.

The fix is a smart switch or dimmer. It replaces the wall switch, so the light works from the wall, the app, and your voice.

That's why we install a lot of Lutron Caseta. The dimmers don't need a neutral wire, so they work in most older homes.

Smart bulbs still have a place. Use them in lamps, or when you want color, like Philips Hue.
