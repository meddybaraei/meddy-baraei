NovaProHome FAQ

Smart home installation questions, answered

Answers about video doorbells, cameras, Ring Alarm, Nest thermostats, smart locks, Lutron lighting and Wi-Fi from NovaProHome in Sterling, Virginia.

General

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. We visit, look at your home and network, and talk through what you want. You get a recommendation with no obligation.

Which areas does NovaProHome serve?

NovaProHome is based in Sterling, Virginia and serves Sterling, Ashburn, Leesburg, Herndon, Reston, Chantilly, South Riding, Centreville, Fairfax, Vienna, McLean and Arlington, plus the rest of Loudoun County, Fairfax County and Northern Virginia.

What certifications does NovaProHome have?

NovaProHome is a Google Nest Pro, a Ring Authorized Partner and a Lutron Preferred Pro.

Can you work with devices I already own?

Usually. We review what you have, keep what works and recommend upgrades only where they make a real difference.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

Not from us. Some manufacturers offer optional cloud plans, such as recorded video history. We explain exactly what each one does so you can decide.

What happens if something stops working?

Call us. You'll talk to the same team that installed your system.

Video Doorbells

Will my existing chime still ring?

Usually yes. We test your chime and transformer first. If either is incompatible, we explain the options before any work starts.

Can you install a video doorbell where there is no doorbell wiring?

Yes. A battery doorbell needs no wiring, or we can run new low-voltage wire when the wall allows it.

Do I need a subscription?

Live view and alerts work without one. Recorded video history usually requires the manufacturer's optional plan, and we explain it before you decide.

How long does a doorbell install take?

Most video doorbell installs take one to two hours, including app setup.

Security Cameras

How many cameras does my house need?

Most single-family homes in our area are well covered with a doorbell plus three to five exterior cameras. We confirm the number during the free assessment.

Can cameras record without the internet?

Wired systems that record to a local recorder keep recording during an internet outage. Cloud cameras need the internet to save video.

Will there be visible wires?

We conceal wiring wherever the structure allows and tell you up front if anything can't be hidden.

Do you install cameras for businesses?

Yes. We cover entrances, registers, stockrooms and parking for offices and small retail.

Ring Alarm

Do I need professional monitoring?

No. Ring Alarm can send alerts to your phone without a plan. Professional monitoring is optional and billed by Ring.

Will pets trigger the motion detectors?

We place and adjust motion detectors to reduce false alarms from pets, and can rely on door sensors in pet areas instead.

Can you add sensors to an existing Ring Alarm?

Yes. We can add sensors, extenders and keypads to a system you already own.

Nest Thermostats

Does a Nest thermostat work with my system?

Most forced-air and heat pump systems are compatible. We confirm before installing and tell you up front if yours needs extra parts.

What if I don't have a C-wire?

We can add one where possible or use the power accessory designed for your system.

Can you install thermostats for multiple zones?

Yes. We install one per zone and set them all up in the same app.

Smart Locks

Will a smart lock fit my door?

Most standard deadbolt doors in our area fit common smart locks. We check measurements first.

What happens if the batteries die?

Most smart locks warn you well in advance, and many keep a physical key or an emergency power option.

Can I give temporary codes to guests or cleaners?

Yes. We show you how to create codes that work only on certain days or times.

Lutron Lighting

Do I need new wiring for smart dimmers?

Usually not. Lutron dimmers replace standard switches in existing boxes.

Caséta or RadioRA 3?

Caséta suits a few rooms. RadioRA 3 suits whole-home systems and renovations with keypads at every entry.

Do my bulbs need to be special?

Use dimmable bulbs. We check your existing fixtures for compatibility.

Whole-Home Wi-Fi

Why is my Wi-Fi slow upstairs or in the basement?

Floors, brick and distance weaken the signal from a single router. Adding properly placed access points fixes it.

Do I need to change internet providers?

Usually not. Most slow-Wi-Fi problems are inside the home, not with the provider.

Can you set up a separate network for smart devices?

Yes. We separate guest and smart-device networks from your personal devices.
