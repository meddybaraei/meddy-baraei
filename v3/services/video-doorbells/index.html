Video Doorbell Installation in Sterling, Ashburn & Northern Virginia

Wired and battery video doorbells installed cleanly, with the chime, transformer and Wi-Fi checked so the doorbell stays online and the button still rings inside.

What's included

  • Doorbell selection for your door frame, power and Wi-Fi
  • Transformer and chime check, with an upgrade when the old one is too weak
  • Angled or wedge mounts when the door sits in a recess
  • Replacement of old intercom or button-only systems
  • App setup, motion zones and alerts for everyone in the house

How we install

  1. Check power. We measure the existing doorbell transformer. Many older homes have one too weak for a video doorbell.
  2. Check Wi-Fi at the door. We test the signal where the doorbell will hang and fix weak spots before mounting.
  3. Mount and wire. The doorbell is leveled, sealed and connected to your existing chime or a new one.
  4. Set it up with you. We set motion zones and alerts on your phone so you are not flooded with notifications.

Advice from our installs

Wired or battery? A wired doorbell never needs recharging and supports more features. Battery models suit doors with no existing doorbell wiring.

Old intercoms. Many NoVA townhomes and older homes still have intercom panels. We can retire them and put a video doorbell in their place.

FAQ

Video Doorbells questions

Will my existing chime still ring?

Usually yes. We test your chime and transformer first. If either is incompatible, we explain the options before any work starts.

Can you install a video doorbell where there is no doorbell wiring?

Yes. A battery doorbell needs no wiring, or we can run new low-voltage wire when the wall allows it.

Do I need a subscription?

Live view and alerts work without one. Recorded video history usually requires the manufacturer's optional plan, and we explain it before you decide.

How long does a doorbell install take?

Most video doorbell installs take one to two hours, including app setup.
