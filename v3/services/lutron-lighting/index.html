What's included

  • Caséta smart dimmers and switches for main living areas
  • RadioRA 3 systems with keypads for larger homes and renovations
  • Scenes such as Good Morning, Movie and Good Night
  • Schedules and away-mode lighting
  • Voice assistant and app setup

How we install

  1. Plan the rooms. We list each room, its switches and how you use the lights.
  2. Choose the system. Caséta or RadioRA 3, based on the size of the project and the keypads you want.
  3. Install. Smart dimmers go into existing switch boxes, with no rewiring in most homes.
  4. Program scenes. We set the scenes and schedules you'll actually use.

FAQ

Lutron Lighting questions

Do I need new wiring for smart dimmers?

Usually not. Lutron dimmers replace standard switches in existing boxes.

Caséta or RadioRA 3?

Caséta suits a few rooms. RadioRA 3 suits whole-home systems and renovations with keypads at every entry.

Do my bulbs need to be special?

Use dimmable bulbs. We check your existing fixtures for compatibility.
