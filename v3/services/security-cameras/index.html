Good to know

Advice from our installs

Wired vs. Wi-Fi cameras. Wired cameras get power and data over one cable and don't need charging. Wi-Fi and battery cameras install anywhere but depend on a strong signal.

Brick and stone. Masonry walls, common in Northern Virginia, weaken Wi-Fi. We test the signal at every mounting spot first.

FAQ

Security Cameras questions

How many cameras does my house need?

Most single-family homes in our area are well covered with a doorbell plus three to five exterior cameras. We confirm the number during the free assessment.

Can cameras record without the internet?

Wired systems that record to a local recorder keep recording during an internet outage. Cloud cameras need the internet to save video.

Will there be visible wires?

We conceal wiring wherever the structure allows and tell you up front if anything can't be hidden.

Do you install cameras for businesses?

Yes. We cover entrances, registers, stockrooms and parking for offices and small retail.
