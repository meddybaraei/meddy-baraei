FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. We visit, look at your home and network, and talk through what you want. You get a recommendation with no obligation.

Can you work with devices I already own?

Usually. We review what you have, keep what works and recommend upgrades only where they make a real difference.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

Not from us. Some manufacturers offer optional cloud plans, such as recorded video history. We explain exactly what each one does so you can decide.

Will there be visible wires?

We conceal wiring wherever the structure allows and tell you up front if anything can't be hidden.

How long does an install take?

A doorbell and a few cameras usually take part of a day. Whole-home Wi-Fi and lighting projects are scheduled over one or more days, and your written plan says how long before we start.

What happens if something stops working?

Call us. You'll talk to the same team that installed your system.
